How soon can I get an appointment?

Most patients are seen within the same week. Urgent cases are worked into the schedule the same day — call the front desk directly for those.

Do I need a referral to see a specialist?

No referral is required. You can book a cardiology, dermatology, or physiotherapy appointment directly through the booking form.

Can I update my health record later?

Yes — open the Health record tab any time, save a new entry, and mention the update to the front desk at your next visit.

What should I bring to my first visit?

A photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medications you're currently taking.
